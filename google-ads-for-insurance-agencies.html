Why most agency ad accounts waste money

Insurance clicks are some of the most expensive in Google Ads. Small setup mistakes add up fast, especially across several offices.

  • Broad keywords with no guardrails: budget goes to "insurance jobs", "claims phone number" and "cheap car insurance online"
  • One campaign for every line: commercial and personal lines fight for the same budget
  • No call tracking: most insurance leads phone in, and untracked calls make good campaigns look bad
  • Homepage landing pages: searchers want a quote for one line in one town, not a general welcome page

Google Ads and SEO work better together

Ads give you search-term data within weeks. We use it to see which lines and towns convert best, then build SEO pages that win those searches without paying per click. Over time you rely less on paid traffic for your best keywords, and every lead that doesn't bind goes into a reactivation sequence.

How much should an insurance agency spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your markets and lines. Most larger agencies start with enough budget per location to get meaningful data in 60 to 90 days, then scale the campaigns that produce quotes at a cost per acquisition you're happy with. We'll recommend a starting budget in your audit.

Can carriers or franchise rules limit my ads?

Yes. Some carriers and captive programs have advertising guidelines. We review those first and build campaigns that stay within them.

Do you work with Local Services Ads?

Yes. Where available for insurance agents in your area, Local Services Ads appear above regular search ads and charge per lead. We handle verification, profile setup and lead disputes.
