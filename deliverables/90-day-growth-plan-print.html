Days 1–14

Phase 1: Build the foundation

Before spending a dollar on ads, make sure every lead can be seen, tracked and followed up. Skipping this step is the most common reason agency marketing "doesn't work".

Set up tracking

Track every quote form and every phone call from your website and Google profiles, so you can see which channel brought each lead.

Tune up each Google Business Profile

Correct categories, services for each line you write, real photos, consistent name, address and phone, and a link to that office's own page.

Start asking for reviews

Send a direct review link by text and email after every new policy and every smooth claim. Make it a habit for the whole team.

Pull your database

Export unbound quotes, lapsed policies and single-policy households from your agency management system. You'll use them in Phase 2.

Days 15–45

Phase 2: Go after quick wins

Now turn on the two channels that can produce quotes fastest: paid search for people ready to buy today, and follow-up with people who already know you.

Launch one focused Google Ads campaign

  • Pick your best line and your strongest office or town
  • Use tight keywords like "[line] insurance [town]" and block job seekers and claims searches
  • Send clicks to a page about that one line, not your homepage
  • Judge it on cost per quote after 30 days, not on clicks

Run your first reactivation test

  • Start small: one segment, such as quotes from the last 6–12 months that didn't bind
  • Send a short, personal email from a real person at your agency, offering a fresh comparison
  • Only text people who have given consent, and always include opt-out
  • Route every reply straight to a producer the same day
Days 46–90

Phase 3: Scale what works

By now you have real numbers. Put more behind the channels producing quotes at a cost you're happy with, and build the long-term assets that keep leads coming without paying per click.

  • Build city and line pages for the searches your ads showed convert best, so you can win them for free over time
  • Expand Google Ads to the next line or office, but only the ones with a good cost per quote
  • Add a cross-sell sequence for single-policy households: auto-only clients get a home or umbrella review, and so on
  • Extend reactivation to lapsed clients, timed 45–60 days before their old renewal date
  • Start a monthly scorecard and review it with your team every month
