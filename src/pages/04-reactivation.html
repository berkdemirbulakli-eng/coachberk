How it works

A done-for-you reactivation engine

We handle the data, copy, sending and routing. Your producers only get the conversations that are ready.

Export and clean

We pull segments from Applied Epic, AMS360, HawkSoft, EZLynx or your CRM, remove duplicates and check consent and do-not-contact status.

Write and sequence

Personal, helpful messages for each segment, delivered by email and SMS, with optional ringless voicemail and call tasks.

Route hot replies

Replies and quote requests go straight to the right producer or office with context attached.

Measure premium

We track replies, quotes, bound policies and premium won back for each segment.

Compliance comes first

Insurance outreach is regulated, and a careless SMS blast can cost far more than it earns. Every campaign is built with:

  • TCPA-aware SMS consent checks and opt-out handling
  • CAN-SPAM compliant email with clear unsubscribe options
  • Respect for state rules and carrier marketing guidelines
  • Your licensed team approving all messaging

CoachBerk is not a law firm. We recommend your agency's counsel review your outreach program.
