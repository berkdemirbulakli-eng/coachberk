FAQ

Questions agency owners ask

Do you only work with insurance agencies?

Yes. CoachBerk works only with insurance agencies, with a focus on multi-producer and multi-location agencies that write personal and commercial lines.

How long does insurance agency SEO take to work?

Google Business Profile and on-page fixes often show movement within 30 to 90 days. Competitive organic rankings for terms like "home insurance near me" usually take 4 to 9 months of consistent work.

What is a reactivation campaign?

A reactivation campaign reaches the people already in your agency management system, such as lapsed policyholders, old quotes and mono-line clients, with compliant email, SMS and call sequences that bring them back to quote or cross-sell.

Do I need Google Ads if I'm investing in SEO?

Most growing agencies run both. Google Ads buys immediate visibility on high-intent searches while SEO builds traffic you don't pay for per click. Search terms data from Ads also tells SEO which pages to build first.
