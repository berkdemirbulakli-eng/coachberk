The four pillars

How we grow organic leads for agencies

📍

1. Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a prospect sees. We optimize each office's categories, services, products, photos, Q&A and posts, and keep your name, address and phone consistent across the directories Google trusts.

🧭

2. Location and line-of-business pages

One page per office and one per core line (auto, home, renters, business, workers' comp, life), each with local proof, carrier partners, FAQs and a clear quote path. These pages target the long-tail searches big carriers ignore.

⭐

3. Reviews and reputation

Review volume, freshness and responses affect local rankings and click-through. We set up review requests after binding and renewal, and templates your team can use to reply.

✍️

4. Helpful content and technical SEO

Answer what policyholders actually ask, like "does homeowners insurance cover a roof leak?", with content written to Google's helpful-content standards. We also fix site speed, mobile issues, schema markup and indexing.

Insurance agency SEO FAQ

How long does SEO take for an insurance agency?

Google Business Profile and on-page fixes often show results in 30 to 90 days. Competitive organic rankings usually take 4 to 9 months. SEO compounds, so the traffic you build keeps paying off long after the work is done.

Can an independent agency outrank big carriers?

For broad national terms, rarely. For local and niche searches, which bring better leads, independent agencies often outrank carriers because Google weighs proximity, relevance and reviews.

Do you write the content?

Yes. We write it and your team reviews it for accuracy and compliance. Insurance content needs to be correct, so a licensed person at your agency always signs off.

Where does your agency rank today?

We'll check your map pack visibility for each office and line, and show you which competitors are taking your searches.
